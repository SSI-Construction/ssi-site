Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial?
Yes — every new admin account starts with a 60-day free trial. No credit card required to begin.
Are there per-project or per-client fees?
No. The $60 CAD/month admin seat includes unlimited projects, budgets, schedules, POs, and invoices.
Do you charge to add my team?
Additional user seats are $20 CAD/month — and free if the user is already an SSI subscriber on their own account.
Can I use SSI Construction Manager outside Canada?
Yes. Pricing is in CAD and Stripe converts to your local currency at checkout. Builders worldwide use SSI.
What's included in the price?
CSI MasterFormat budgeting, schedules with dependencies and Gantt charts, purchase orders, invoice tracking, PDF reports, look-ahead schedules with email reminders, and priority support.
