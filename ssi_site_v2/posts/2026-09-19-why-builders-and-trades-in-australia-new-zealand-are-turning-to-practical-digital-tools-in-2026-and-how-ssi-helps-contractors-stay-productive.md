## **The Productivity Reset Happening Across AUS/NZ**

The construction sector in Australia and New Zealand is undergoing a major productivity reset. Output per labour hour has fallen sharply over the past three decades, with Australia seeing a 53% decline and New Zealand showing similar patterns . Builders are working longer hours, paying more for materials, and managing tighter schedules — yet delivering fewer homes per labour hour.

This decline has triggered a shift toward:

* cloud‑based project management
* digital documentation
* structured scheduling
* real‑time field reporting
* modular and data‑driven delivery

Digital tools are no longer optional — they’re becoming the operational backbone of efficient construction.

AI‑assisted design, autonomous equipment, and automated surveying are emerging across AUS/NZ construction sites . Builders need digital foundations to integrate these tools over time.
