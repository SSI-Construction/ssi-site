Construction companies across Canada are facing tighter margins, rising labour costs, and increasing pressure to deliver projects with greater accuracy and transparency. While large enterprise builders often rely on complex, expensive software ecosystems, small‑to‑midsize general contractors and trade subcontractors need something different: **simple setup, predictable pricing, and tools that actually match their day‑to‑day workflow.**

SSI Construction Manager and SSI Trade Manager were built specifically for this segment of the industry. Instead of competing with enterprise platforms, SSI focuses on clarity, affordability, and seamless collaboration between GCs and trades — all without the heavy overhead of legacy systems.

Below is why SSI is becoming the preferred construction management software for growing contractors in 2026.

## **Transparent Pricing Designed for Real Contractors**

Most construction software platforms rely on aggressive seat‑based pricing, annual contracts, or project‑volume billing. SSI takes the opposite approach.

### **SSI Pricing Structure**

* **SSI Construction Manager:** ~$60 CAD/month per admin seat
* **Additional users:** ~$20 CAD/month
* **SSI Trade Manager:** Included at no extra charge when collaborating with active SSI subscribers
* **Crew Time Clock add‑on:** ~$25 CAD/month
* **60‑day trial:** No credit card required

This flat‑rate model gives contractors predictable monthly costs without forcing them into expensive tiers or multi‑year commitments.

Large platforms like Procore and Autodesk Construction Cloud are powerful, but they’re built for enterprise builders with IT departments, multi‑office operations, and complex governance requirements. Their pricing often ranges from **$5,000 to $20,000+ USD per year**, making them unrealistic for smaller firms.

SSI fills the gap by offering:

* Fast setup
* Straightforward workflows
* Essential project controls
* No implementation fees
* No forced training packages
* No project‑volume penalties

Contractors can be fully operational in under an hour.

### **Contractor Foreman**

A value‑focused platform with dozens of modules. **Where SSI wins:** Cleaner interface, faster onboarding, and simpler workflows for budgeting, scheduling, POs, and trade collaboration.

### **Fieldwire**

Strong for blueprint markups and field coordination. **Where SSI wins:** Better cost tracking, job costing, and predictable pricing as teams grow.

### **Buildertrend**

Popular with homebuilders and remodelers. **Where SSI wins:** Lower cost footprint and no feature bloat for contractors who don’t need client selection portals.

### **Procore / Autodesk Construction Cloud**

Enterprise‑grade systems with deep integrations. **Where SSI wins:** Affordability, simplicity, and suitability for small‑to‑midsize contractors who find enterprise platforms overwhelming.

### **1. Straightforward Project Scheduling**

GCs can build schedules, assign tasks, and track progress without navigating complex menus or configuration screens.

Budgets tie directly to:

* Purchase orders
* Invoices
* Change orders

This creates a clear financial trail from start to finish.

### **3. Document & Drawing Management**

GCs upload drawings, revisions, and specs. Trades always see the latest version — no outdated plans, no confusion.

### **4. Purchase Order Tracking**

POs connect directly to budgets and cost codes, giving contractors real‑time visibility into committed costs.

### **5. Change Order Management**

### **1. Crew Time Clock With GPS**

Accurate, verified timesheets without paperwork.

### **2. Job Tracking & Daily Logs**

Trades upload photos, notes, completed tasks, and material usage — all visible to the GC instantly.

### **3. Inventory & Tool Tracking**

Reduce lost tools, duplicate purchases, and unbilled materials.

### **4. Faster Invoicing**

### **5. Master Schedule for All Crews**

## **A Shared Ecosystem That Connects GCs and Trades**

This is SSI’s strongest advantage.

Both SSI Construction Manager and SSI Trade Manager run on the **same unified platform**, allowing GCs and trades to collaborate without syncing files or using separate systems.

### **What this means for contractors**

* GCs invite trades directly into projects
* Trades instantly see drawings, schedules, and change orders
* GCs receive real‑time updates from the field
* Trades submit timesheets, photos, and progress directly
* Everyone works from the same source of truth

This eliminates miscommunication, reduces admin time, and keeps projects moving smoothly.

* 3D/BIM model rendering

* Deep enterprise integrations
* Client design‑selection portals

Contractors who need these specialized features may prefer Fieldwire, Autodesk, or Buildertrend.
