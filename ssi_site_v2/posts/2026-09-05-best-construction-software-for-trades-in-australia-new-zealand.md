Trade subcontractors — electricians, plumbers, HVAC, concrete, carpentry, roofing, and more — are facing increasing pressure to deliver accurate work, manage labour efficiently, and keep up with builder expectations. The right trade management software can dramatically improve productivity and profitability.

Here are the best construction software options for trades in 2026, and why SSI Trade Manager is becoming a preferred choice.

## **1. SSI Trade Manager (Best Overall for Trades)**

SSI Trade Manager is designed specifically for trade subcontractors who need simple scheduling, job tracking, and labour management. It integrates directly with SSI Construction Manager, allowing trades to collaborate with builders on the same platform.

### **Key Benefits for Trades**

* GPS‑verified crew time clock
* Automatic timesheet generation
* Job tracking with photos and notes
* Material and inventory tracking
* Faster invoicing
* Master schedule for all crews
* Direct collaboration with builders

Trades can reduce admin time and increase billable hours without learning a complex system.

## **2. Fieldwire**

Fieldwire is strong for trades who work heavily off drawings and need tight coordination with builders.

### **Best For**

* Electrical
* Mechanical
* Fire protection
* Concrete

## **3. Simpro**

Simpro is widely used in Australia and New Zealand for service‑based trades. It offers quoting, dispatching, and job management tools.

### **Best For**

* Service trades
* Maintenance contractors

## **4. Contractor Foreman**

Contractor Foreman offers a broad set of modules that can benefit trades needing more operational tools.

### **Best For**

* Trades wanting CRM, safety, fleet tracking
* Multi‑crew trade companies

## **Why SSI Stands Out for Trades**

Trades need:

* Simple job tracking
* Accurate labour management
* Fast invoicing
* Clear communication with builders

SSI Trade Manager delivers all of this with a clean interface and seamless integration with SSI Construction Manager, allowing trades to work directly inside builder‑managed projects.

## **Conclusion**

For trades across Australia and New Zealand, SSI Trade Manager offers one of the strongest solutions in 2026. It provides simple scheduling, accurate labour tracking, and seamless collaboration with builders — all at a predictable monthly cost.
