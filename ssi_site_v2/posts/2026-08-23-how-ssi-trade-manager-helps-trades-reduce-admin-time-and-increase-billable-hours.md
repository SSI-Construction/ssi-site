The trade industry is under more pressure than ever in 2026. Electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, framers, concrete crews, and specialty trades are all dealing with tighter schedules, rising labour costs, and unpredictable project pipelines. But one challenge stands out above the rest:

**Too much admin time. Not enough billable time.**

Between paperwork, scheduling, timesheets, job tracking, inventory management, and client communication, trade companies often lose hours every week to tasks that don’t generate revenue.

SSI Trade Manager was built to solve exactly that problem — and because it runs on the same platform as SSI Construction Manager, trades also gain a powerful collaboration advantage when working with general contractors.

Below is a breakdown of how SSI Trade Manager helps trade companies reduce admin time, streamline operations, and increase billable hours — all while improving accuracy, accountability, and profitability.

## **1. Centralized Job Management (No More Scattered Information)**

Trade companies often juggle:

* Text messages
* Paper notes
* Emails
* Spreadsheets
* Whiteboards

SSI Trade Manager replaces all of that with a **single job dashboard** where trades can:

* Create jobs
* Assign crews
* Track progress
* Upload documents
* Store photos
* Manage change orders

This eliminates hours of back‑and‑forth communication and keeps every job organized from start to finish.

**Result:** Less admin time. Faster job setup. Fewer mistakes.

## **2. Master Scheduling Across All Active Jobs**

Trades rarely work on one job at a time. SSI Trade Manager’s **Master Schedule** gives companies a real‑time view of:

* Crew availability
* Job timelines
* Overlapping tasks
* Daily and weekly workload

Instead of manually coordinating schedules, the system automatically updates as jobs progress.

**Result:** Less time spent scheduling. More time spent working.

## **3. Crew Time Clock With GPS Verification**

One of the biggest admin burdens for trade companies is collecting accurate timesheets.

SSI Trade Manager solves this with:

* Mobile clock‑in/clock‑out
* GPS verification
* Photo capture
* Automatic timesheet generation
* Cost‑code tracking

This eliminates:

* Manual timesheet entry
* Missing hours
* Incorrect job coding
* Payroll disputes

**Result:** Admin time drops dramatically. Billable hours become accurate and defensible.

## **4. Real‑Time Cost Tracking and Budget Visibility**

Trade companies often lose money because they don’t know:

* How many hours were spent on a job
* How much material was used
* Whether the job is still profitable

SSI Trade Manager links:

* Labour
* Materials
* Purchase orders
* Invoices

…directly to each job.

This gives trades real‑time visibility into job profitability without digging through spreadsheets.

**Result:** Better financial decisions. More profitable jobs.

## **5. Inventory and Material Tracking**

Material loss is a silent profit killer.

SSI Trade Manager helps trades track:

* Tools
* Consumables
* Equipment
* Job‑specific materials

This reduces:

* Lost inventory
* Duplicate purchases
* Unbilled materials

**Result:** More billable material. Less waste.

## **6. Faster Invoicing and Fewer Payment Delays**

Admin bottlenecks often delay invoicing — which delays cash flow.

SSI Trade Manager streamlines:

* Progress billing
* Change order billing
* Labour and material summaries
* Invoice generation

With everything already tracked inside the system, invoices can be created in minutes instead of hours.

**Result:** Faster payments. Stronger cash flow.

## **7. Field‑to‑Office Communication Without Friction**

Trade companies lose time when field crews and office staff aren’t aligned.

SSI Trade Manager connects them through:

* Real‑time updates
* Job notes
* Photo uploads
* Task completion tracking
* Automated notifications

This eliminates unnecessary phone calls and manual updates.

**Result:** Less admin time. More productive crews.
